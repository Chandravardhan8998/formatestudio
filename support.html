What to include in a bug report

  • Your macOS version.
  • Your Format Studio version and build number.
  • The file format involved, such as JSON, YAML, XML, CSV, SQLite, Parquet, HAR, or another supported type.
  • The steps you took before the issue happened.
  • Whether the issue happens again with a small synthetic sample.
Please protect confidential data.
Do not email production credentials, private keys, access tokens, customer data, or other sensitive files. If a sample is necessary, please use a sanitized or synthetic version whenever possible.
