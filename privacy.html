Privacy Policy

Effective: September 3, 2026

Format Studio is designed as a local-first macOS developer utility. This policy explains how the app handles files, clipboard content, local storage, subscriptions, and other information.

1. Local file processing

Files you open in Format Studio are processed locally on your Mac. The app does not upload the contents of your developer files to our servers for parsing, editing, inspection, comparison, conversion, validation, transformation, or search.

If you choose to open a file stored in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, another File Provider, or a network-backed location, that storage provider may synchronize the file independently of Format Studio.

2. Clipboard and selected text

Format Studio reads clipboard text only after an explicit user action, such as Paste, Inspect Clipboard, or a macOS service action you invoke.

The app does not continuously monitor the clipboard and does not maintain a persistent clipboard history.

3. Sensitive developer data

Developer files can contain sensitive information such as environment variables, API tokens, Authorization headers, cookies, JWTs, certificates, private keys, database contents, and query parameters. Format Studio is designed to process this information locally.

The app does not intentionally send these document contents to our servers or third-party advertising or analytics services.

4. Local recovery, preferences, and temporary data

Format Studio may store limited information locally on your Mac to provide normal app functionality, including:

  • Application preferences and UI settings.
  • Recent-document access information and security-scoped bookmarks where needed by macOS.
  • Local recovery data for unsaved editable documents, to help prevent data loss.
  • Temporary caches or indexes used for large-file inspection and performance.

These items remain local to the app's macOS storage/container and are not used to build advertising profiles. Temporary and recovery data is cleaned according to the app's lifecycle and cache policies.

5. Clipboard, private keys, and passwords

Format Studio does not persist a clipboard history. Passwords used to inspect password-protected P12/PKCS#12 files are not intentionally stored as application preferences, analytics, or account data.

Private-key material is treated as sensitive and is not intentionally uploaded or logged by the app.

6. Network access

Core document workflows do not require a Format Studio cloud service. The app does not automatically fetch remote OpenAPI or JSON Schema references, JWT key references, remote certificate resources, or remote content for local document previews as part of normal local inspection.

If you choose an external link such as Support, Privacy, or subscription-management information, macOS or your web browser may access that destination at your request.

7. Subscriptions and Apple StoreKit

Format Studio Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the Mac App Store and StoreKit. Apple may process purchase, billing, storefront, and account information according to Apple's own privacy practices.

Format Studio does not require a separate Format Studio account to use normal local document functionality. Developer document contents are not sent to StoreKit as part of subscription entitlement checks.

8. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Format Studio does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. The app does not use developer-document contents for advertising.

If this behavior changes in a future version, this policy and the applicable App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

9. Crash diagnostics

macOS and Apple may provide system-level diagnostics depending on your device and Apple settings. Format Studio does not use those system diagnostics as permission to intentionally log sensitive document contents.

10. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your developer-file contents or clipboard contents. We do not share them with advertisers or data brokers.

11. Data retention

Because core developer documents are processed locally, Format Studio does not maintain a server-side archive of those files. Local preferences, recovery information, bookmarks, and caches remain on your Mac until removed by the app, by you, or by macOS according to their normal lifecycle.

12. Children

Format Studio is a professional developer utility and is not designed as a children's service. The app does not provide social networking, messaging, advertising, or user-generated content hosting.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Format Studio's functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the current version.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or other inquiries, contact: vardhanapps@gmail.com.
